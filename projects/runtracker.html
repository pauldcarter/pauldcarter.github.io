RunTracker

RunTracker presents parkrun results as visualised progress over time. Results are imported in a single step, then displayed in interactive charts that can be shared via read-only links.

Visit live ↗

Getting your results in takes one click

Open your parkrun results page, click once, and your history is in. There is nothing to type in by hand and no spreadsheet to keep up to date — and the charts that come out can be shared with grandparents on a link, without them signing up for anything.

Key features

  • One-click import straight from your parkrun results page — no typing anything in.
  • Charts of times, finishing positions and improvement over the years.
  • Track the whole family together and compare runners side by side.
  • Share a link so others can see the results without creating an account.
  • Works on phones as well as desktop.
