Golf Coach

A golf coaching application in which a coach curates a visual knowledge base of technique — foundations, symptoms and fixes — and the AI is restricted to recommending only from that approved library.

In development

The coach decides what the AI is allowed to say

A coach curates the library of technique — foundations, symptoms and fixes — and the app recommends only from that approved material. Every piece of advice a golfer receives traces back to a coach they can name.

Key features

  • A visual mind-map editor for building and rearranging the coaching library.
  • Diagnoses a golfer's fault from the symptoms they describe.
  • Prescribes only fixes the coach has approved — never invented advice.
  • Accounts for compensations, so it treats the cause rather than the symptom.
  • Separate views for the coach editing the library and the golfer using it.
