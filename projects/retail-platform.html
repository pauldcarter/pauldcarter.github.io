Retail Platform

A retail system running a real shop: an online store, a staffed till and a self-serve kiosk, all selling from the same stock and taking card payments.

Visit live ↗

One stock list behind all three tills

Sell the last item online and it disappears from the shop-floor till the same moment. Because every channel draws on the same stock, nothing gets sold twice and there are no separate systems to reconcile at the end of the day.

Key features

  • Online shop, staffed till and self-serve kiosk, all selling the same stock.
  • Items are held as soon as they go in a basket, so the last one can't be sold twice.
  • Card payments online and in person, including card readers in the shop.
  • Customers sign in with an email address or with Google.
  • UK address lookup at checkout — enter a postcode and pick the address.
