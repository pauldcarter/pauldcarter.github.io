Retail Platform

A retail system providing three sales channels — an online store, a staffed till and a self-serve kiosk — running on a single codebase against one stock pool, with card payments and Google sign-in.

Visit live ↗

One stock pool, three channels

The web, till and kiosk channels share the same stock, with each channel handling reservation according to its own risk profile: the web channel reserves stock at the basket, the till relies on the staff member, and the kiosk uses a simplified flow. This prevents double-selling and avoids reconciling separate systems.

Key features

  • Three sales channels — web shop, staffed till and self-serve kiosk — on one codebase and one stock pool.
  • Reserve-at-basket stock handling on the web channel, preventing two customers from purchasing the same remaining item.
  • Card payments and reader integration via SumUp, covering online and in-person sales.
  • Sign-in with email or Google via django-allauth; UK address lookup via postcodes.io.
  • Product media served from object storage (R2), reducing load on the virtual machine.
