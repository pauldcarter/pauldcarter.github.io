Family Manager

An app for organising family life. It installs to your home screen, opens like any other app, and keeps working when you have no signal.

Visit live ↗

Your family's details never leave your phone

There is no account to create and nowhere for your data to be sent. Everything you enter stays on your own device, which means the app works just as well on a train with no signal as it does at home.

Key features

  • Installs to your home screen on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Works fully offline — no signal needed.
  • No account, no sign-up, no password to remember.
  • Family details stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • Free to use, with no adverts and nothing to subscribe to.
