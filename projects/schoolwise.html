Schoolwise

Schoolwise presents UK school performance over multiple years rather than as single-year league tables, showing longer-term trajectories and allowing search by postcode on an interactive map.

Visit live ↗

A trend tells you more than a ranking

One year's league table can turn on a single unusual cohort. Seeing a school's results across several years shows whether good marks are a pattern or a one-off — and every figure shown comes straight from the published government data, unaltered.

Key features

  • Several years of results per school, so you can see the trend rather than one snapshot.
  • Search by postcode on a map to find the schools near you.
  • Compare nearby schools against each other on the same measures.
  • Every figure comes straight from the official government data, unchanged.
  • Free to use with no sign-in — updated as each year's data is published.
