Learning Lab

A learning site for children bringing together mental maths, spelling and story writing, alongside a live multiplayer game of Perudo (liar's dice) to play against family and friends.

Visit live ↗

Practice and play in the same place

Most learning sites stop at drills. Here the quiet practice apps sit next to a live game children actually want to play, so the same sign-in covers both the maths homework and the reward afterwards.

Key features

  • Mental maths practice that adapts as a child works through it.
  • Spelling practice with word lists to work through.
  • A story-writing app for creative work.
  • Live multiplayer Perudo — play against family and friends in real time.
  • One Google sign-in for everything, on any device with a browser.
