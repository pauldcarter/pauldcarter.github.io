Learning Lab

A learning site for children aged roughly 6 to 11, covering times tables, multiplication, addition and fractions, alongside spelling practice and story writing — plus Perudo, a dice bluffing game. Free, with no adverts.

Times tables that notice what a child finds hard

Rather than asking every fact equally often, the times tables game watches which ones a child gets wrong or answers slowly, and asks those more. Facts answered quickly and correctly turn green, then gold, and drop out of rotation — so practice keeps moving to the tables that still need it.

Key features

  • Times tables practice that concentrates on the facts a child keeps missing.
  • Multiplication, addition and fractions, taught visually rather than as bare sums.
  • Spelling practice that tracks each word and retires the ones a child has mastered.
  • A story-writing app, with saved stories to read back later.
  • Perudo, a dice bluffing game, against computer opponents at three difficulty levels or against other people.
  • A coaching mode in Perudo that explains what the odds actually favour.
  • Free with no adverts; sign in with Google to save progress across devices.
