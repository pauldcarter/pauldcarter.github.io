CoursesPlayed

CoursesPlayed puts every golf course you have played on a map. Rounds are tagged with the people you played with, and each player can see how far they have got through the published top-100 course lists.

Visit live ↗

Your playing partners' courses, on the same map

Tag who you played with and they get a single email asking them to confirm. Once they do, each of you sees the other's courses on your own map, and can filter it down to the courses you have played together.

Key features

  • Over 9,000 golf clubs in more than 130 countries, built from OpenStreetMap, with multi-course venues split into their individual courses.
  • Search by club, town, county or country, tolerant of misspellings.
  • Progress against seven top-100 lists from five publishers, covering the UK & Ireland, Europe, the USA and the world.
  • Partner tagging with confirmation by email. Partners can be added by name alone, with an email added later.
  • A private 1–5 rating for each course, and filters for location, country, list and partner.
  • Google sign-in, and works on phones as well as desktop.
