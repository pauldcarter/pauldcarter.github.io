Golf Tips DB

A library of golf instruction videos, tagged by the problem they address, the solution they propose and the shot they apply to — so it is searched by what you are doing wrong rather than by who filmed it.

In development Visit live ↗

The fastest way to find the tip you are looking for

A saved video is only worth saving if it can be found again. Rather than a list of titles to scroll, a golfer types the miss — thin from a bunker, a slice off the tee — and lands on what the library actually proposes for it, counted from the videos tagged with that fault. The search resolves everyday words onto the vocabulary, so it finds the tip without knowing the term a coach would use for it.

Key features

  • Search that resolves what a golfer types onto the vocabulary, so everyday words find the right fault.
  • Videos tagged with the faults they fix, the goals they teach and the setup or swing changes they propose.
  • A work-on list counted from the tagged videos, with a coach's notes over the top of it.
  • A private library for each member, where they import and tag their own videos.
  • Bulk import that takes a whole playlist at once and pre-ticks tags from each title.
  • A curator's admin where every edit is live the moment it saves — no spreadsheet and no publish step.
